About my study

After entering this university, I have systematically studied the legal system of Kazakhstan. I have gradually learned to understand legal institutions from different perspectives and think about the relationship between law and real society, and I have gained a lot.

1. Criminal policy vs. criminal law

Criminal policy is the broader strategy for preventing and responding to crime (prevention, enforcement, sentencing, rehabilitation). Criminal law is the legal tool that defines offences and penalties.

2. Is criminalisation always effective?

No. Criminal law should be a last resort. Over-criminalisation can overload courts and prisons and ignore root causes like poverty or addiction. Administrative, civil or social measures are sometimes better.

3. Financial legal norms

Financial legal norms are the state's rules for how public money is raised, distributed, and used. They are mostly imperative: the state is the dominant party, and violations bring monetary sanctions that the state can often impose without going to court. Each norm has a hypothesis, a disposition, and a sanction. The Constitution is the top source of financial law, and below it come the Tax Code, other laws, decrees, government resolutions, and local acts.
